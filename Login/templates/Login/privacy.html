Privacy Policy

How we protect and handle your information

Last updated: May 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Student Companion. We understand that mental health information is deeply personal and sensitive. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our AI-powered mental wellness platform.

By using our service, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the platform.

2. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account information: Email address, name, and password (encrypted) when you sign up
  • Assessment responses: Answers to mental health questionnaires (PHQ-9, GAD-7, PSS, etc.)
  • Self-reported feelings: Free-text descriptions of your mental state
  • Optional facial emotion data: If you choose to use the camera-based emotion analysis feature

Information Collected Automatically

  • Session timestamps and duration
  • Device and browser information
  • IP address (used for security only)
  • Usage patterns within the platform

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely to:

  • Provide personalized mental wellness assessments and recommendations
  • Track your progress over time and show meaningful insights
  • Generate AI-assisted Mental Status Examination (MSE) reports
  • Improve the accuracy of our wellness models (using anonymized data only)
  • Detect signs of crisis to direct you to emergency resources
  • Communicate important account or service updates

4. AI and Automated Analysis

Student Companion uses AI to analyze your responses and provide insights. Important:

  • AI analysis is assistive, not diagnostic. It does not replace a qualified mental health professional.
  • AI models are trained on anonymized, aggregated data — never on your individual identifiable information.
  • You can request that your data not be used for model improvement at any time by contacting us.

5. Data Security

We take the security of your mental health data seriously:

  • All data is encrypted in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and at rest (AES-256)
  • Passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms
  • Access to user data is restricted to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis
  • We conduct regular security audits and follow best practices for healthcare-adjacent data

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. Ever.

We share information only in these limited cases:

  • With your consent — for example, if you choose to share a report with a counselor
  • Crisis situations — if there is imminent risk of harm, we may direct you to emergency services
  • Legal requirements — if required by Pakistani law or court order
  • Service providers — secure infrastructure providers (e.g., cloud hosting) bound by strict confidentiality agreements

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access all your data we hold
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and all associated data permanently
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Opt out of having anonymized data used for model improvement
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact privacy@studentcompanion.pk.

8. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all personally identifiable information is permanently removed within 30 days. Anonymized aggregate data may be retained for research and service improvement.

9. Minors

Student Companion is intended for users aged 16 and above. If you are under 18, we encourage you to use the platform with the awareness of a parent, guardian, or trusted adult.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via email and by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
